FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key concerns before booking: lead time, trial stays, updates, medication, emergency handling, and service area.

Ask About My Cat
How early should I book?

We recommend 7-14 days in advance. For holiday windows, 3-4 weeks is safer.

Can we do a trial stay first?

Yes. Short trial stays are recommended for first-time or sensitive cats.

How often do you send updates?

At least one detailed update daily with photos/videos and feeding/litter notes.

Do you support medication?

Routine oral medication support is available. Advanced medical cases are assessed case by case.

Will my cat be mixed with others?

Only when temperament and safety fit. Separate care zones are used when needed.

How are emergencies handled?

We contact owners immediately and follow the pre-approved clinic and authorization plan.

Do you offer pickup/drop-off?

Yes, for selected routes in Downtown Vancouver, Burnaby, and Richmond.

What is your cancellation policy?

Usually full refund before 72 hours. Within 72 hours, partial reservation fees may apply.

What should we bring for boarding?

Bring food, treats, meds, and familiar comfort items. We provide tofu litter and a litter box, plus basic bowls if needed. If your cat only uses wood litter, please bring your own.

Do you offer multi-cat rates?

Yes. Bundled rates depend on care complexity and stay length.

How fast do you reply?

Typically within 1-3 hours during service hours.

Vancouver Cat Care Checklist

Before booking any cat sitter in Vancouver, confirm three things: update cadence, emergency handling clarity, and written pricing boundaries.
