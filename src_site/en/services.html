Add-ons

  • Holiday periods may increase base pricing by 10%-20%.
  • Multi-cat households can receive bundled pricing.
  • Special care requests are assessed case by case.
  • Urgent or late-night requests may include rush fees.

Cancellation Policy

  • Usually full refund when canceled 72+ hours in advance.
  • Within 72 hours, partial reservation fees may apply.
  • Early return is billed for completed and reserved time.
  • Emergency weather disruptions are handled by rebooking priority.

Not sure which service fits?

If your cat is sensitive to environment changes, start with a short trial. If your cat prefers familiar surroundings, drop-in visits are often better.
