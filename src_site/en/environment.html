Environment

Boarding Space and Daily Routine

This is a routine-based care setup, not passive pet holding. Space zoning, hygiene discipline, and behavior logging reduce stress and improve visibility for cat parents.

  • Quiet zoning
  • Daily cleaning
  • Behavior logs
  • Emergency escalation

Sample Daily Flow

08:00-10:00

Morning check, feeding, hydration, and status logging.

12:00-15:00

Interactive play, grooming, litter reset, and activity observation.

19:00-21:00

Evening calm routine, second cleaning pass, and owner update.

Safety and Hygiene

  • Basic health and vaccine checks before intake.
  • Grouped bowls/litter tools to reduce cross-contact risk.
  • Daily high-touch disinfection and ventilation schedule.
  • Escalation messaging for continuous appetite/mood/litter anomalies.
  • Pre-aligned emergency clinic and owner authorization plan.
